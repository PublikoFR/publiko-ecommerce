Surface container for grouped content. Pair with `CardHeader` for a titled block.

```jsx
<Card elevation="md" interactive accent>
  <CardHeader title="Field 4 — North" subtitle="32 ha · Wheat" action={<Badge tone="success" dot>Healthy</Badge>} />
  <p>Soil moisture is optimal across the parcel.</p>
</Card>
```

`padding`: none/sm/md/lg · `elevation`: none/sm/md/lg · `interactive` lifts on hover · `accent` adds a lime top bar.
